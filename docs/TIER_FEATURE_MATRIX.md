## Upgrade Paths

- Free users hit limits through hook generation, scheduling, watermark, or premium template locks.
- Standard users upgrade when they need 4K export, unlimited scheduling, multi-account, or intro/outro video branding.
- Premium users may choose Lifetime while early slots remain.
